Parents ask

Common questions.

Which classes are available?

Nursery, LKG, UKG and Class 1 to Class 7. Admissions for the 2027–28 session are open for all classes, subject to seat availability.

What is the medium of instruction?

English medium, with Hindi taught as a full subject. Teachers explain in Hindi wherever a young child needs it.

What is the right age for Nursery?

Children who are around 3 years old at the start of the session can join Nursery. LKG is for about 4 years, UKG for about 5 years and Class 1 for about 6 years.

How can I know the fee structure?

Call or WhatsApp the school office, or visit the campus. We explain the full fees with no hidden charges.

Is transport available?

Please ask the school office about van routes covering Areraj and nearby villages.
